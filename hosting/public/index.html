How HomeShelf works

  1. Capture a reference photo of the storage (shelf, cabinet, fridge, etc).
  2. Optionally draw a grid to help align future scans — add a line with +H or +V, select it, drag to position, or delete it. Save the final grid.
  3. Mark the items on the shelf — tap the photo to create an item, then drag, rename, or pinch to resize. Save when done.
  4. Grid and items can also be generated automatically — tap the auto button, then review and edit as needed.
  5. Later, scanning the same storage aligns and compares it against the reference photo.
  6. On the review screen, color coding tells you what changed: per-cell similarity badges are green (≥90%), yellow (≥70%), or red (below). After AI analysis, change boxes are green = added, red = removed/consumed, yellow = partially consumed, gray = unknown.
